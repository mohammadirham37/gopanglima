# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want goPanglima to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or other conduct that would reasonably be
  considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when representing the project publicly.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions, or temporarily or
permanently ban contributors, for behavior they deem inappropriate.

Report incidents privately to the maintainer through GitHub
(<https://github.com/mohammadirham37>). All reports will be reviewed and kept
confidential.
